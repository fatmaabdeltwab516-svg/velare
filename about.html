“A jewel is not an ornament of display; it is an intimate monument to unhurried time.”

Born in the lapidary quarters of Cairo and trained under the ancestral gemological masters of the Nile basin, Marc Vélara spent thirty seasons seeking stones that conventional cutters cast aside: unheated corundum with celestial inclusions, raw emerald crystals bearing the weight of geological centuries.

In 1998, he established his first private bench overlooking the ancient river, devoted to single-origin talismans commissioned exclusively by patrons who understood that luxury is measured not in carats, but in silence.

Today, between the historic salons of Place Vendôme and the quiet workshops of Cairo, every VÉLARA artifact passes through his personal hands—tested against ancestral tension calibrations before receiving the sovereign hallmark.

ARCHIVAL PRINCIPLES

The Vélara Creed

Slowness as Sovereign 01

We produce fewer than twenty-five sovereign pieces each year. A single tension-mount bezel demands sixty hours of optical alignment; we refuse the accelerations of mechanized casting.

Unheated Provenance 02

Only earth-born, unheated sapphires, un- oiled emeralds, and conflict-free river diamonds enter our vault. Every stone carries dual gemological attestations from Gübelin and GIA.

Repair over Replace 03

Every artifact is recorded in the permanent Maison Registry with lifelong stewardship. We maintain ancestral alloys to re-burnish, recalibrate, and preserve your jewel across three generations.
