THE ARCHIVAL METHOD

Three Steps to Permanence

01 — The Sitting

A 45-minute conversation at the atelier — no sketch required. We listen to the provenance and spirit of your vision.

02 — The Design

We return with an initial rendering for your approval, balancing structural gemology with sculptural harmony.

03 — The Crafting

Your piece is hand-finished over several weeks by our master goldsmiths and certified in the Maison Registry.
